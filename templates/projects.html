Adversarial ML research

As part of a 9-week NSF REU at the University of Nebraska Omaha's ASTAR Lab under Dr. Peng Jiang, I'm studying adversarial attacks on cross-view geo-localization — building each attack from first principles on standard datasets before bringing it to a satellite-image retrieval pipeline. Fast Gradient Sign Method — a single step along the sign of the loss gradient that reliably fools the classifier while staying inside an ε budget.

Projected Gradient Descent — the iterative, multi-step successor to FGSM and the standard strong first-order attack. Plots accuracy vs. ε.

A privacy attack that perturbs an image so a vision-language model (LLaVA) can no longer guess where it was taken — maximizing geographic error through CLIP gradients while leaving the photo visually unchanged.
