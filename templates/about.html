Research

In the summer of 2026 I took part in a 9-week NSF Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) at the University of Nebraska Omaha's ASTAR Lab, under Dr. Peng Jiang, on the adversarial-attack track. I studied how adversarial attacks break cross-view geo-localization models. I implemented FGSM and PGD from scratch, trained my own MNIST and CIFAR-10 CNNs to use as white-box targets, and plotted how accuracy collapsed as the perturbation budget grew. I also built GeoShield, a privacy attack that perturbs an image through CLIP gradients so a vision-language model can no longer tell where a photo was taken, while leaving it visually unchanged. The code behind all of it is on my projects page.
