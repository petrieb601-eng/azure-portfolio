Making a geo-localization model lose its place

Cross-view geo-localization matches a ground photo against satellite tiles to say where it was taken. Working on the IARPA WRIVA program under Dr. Peng Jiang, I attacked Sample4Geo, a model that scores 98.68% Recall@1 on CVUSA, with perturbations too small to see.

Try the attack

Drag the slider to strengthen the perturbation. The photo looks the same to you, but the model's guess moves to a different site.

This is an illustration of the idea. The measured results are below.
