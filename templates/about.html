About me

I'm a Software Engineering and Information Technology student at William Penn University with a 3.9 GPA. As a dual-sport athlete in soccer and track, I've built the discipline, time management and teamwork that carry straight into how I build software.

My work spans cybersecurity, full-stack web development, embedded systems and AI integration, and most of it ends up as something you can run, like the tools on this site.

In summer 2026 I joined a 9-week NSF Research Experience for Undergraduates at the University of Nebraska Omaha's ASTAR Lab, under Dr. Peng Jiang, on the adversarial-attack track. I implemented FGSM and PGD from scratch, trained my own MNIST and CIFAR-10 models as white-box targets, and built GeoShield, a privacy attack that stops a vision-language model from telling where a photo was taken. The code is on my projects page.

I've also worked as a Lab Assistant at William Penn, helping students troubleshoot software and programming problems, and in food service, where I trained new employees.
