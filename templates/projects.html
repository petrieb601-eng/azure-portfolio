CIFAR-10 CNN

Model

A deeper CNN trained on CIFAR-10, three channels and ten classes, to test how the same attacks behave on natural images.

PyTorch, 2 conv and 2 fc layers, 10 classes

train_cifar10.py

FGSM

Attack

Fast Gradient Sign Method: a single step along the sign of the loss gradient that reliably fools the classifier while staying inside an ε budget.

Single step, sign(∇loss), L∞

fgsm_cifar10.py

PGD

Attack

Projected Gradient Descent, the iterative successor to FGSM and the standard strong first-order attack. Plots accuracy against ε.

Iterative, multi-step, L∞ projection

pgd_cifar10.py

GeoShield

Privacy

A privacy attack that perturbs an image so a vision-language model (LLaVA) can no longer guess where it was taken, maximizing geographic error through CLIP gradients while leaving the photo visually unchanged.

CLIP ViT-B/32, LLaVA 13B, 200-step PGD

geoshield_attack.py
